OVERVIEW
Jason Kwame Asiedu is a New York-based designer and maker working across architecture, fashion, textiles and materials, UX/UI, graphic design, photography, publication and editorial, exhibitions, stage design, film and video, and event programming. Graduated from Carnegie Mellon Univeristy with a degree in Architecture and Human computer interaction. Currently a master's student in Design Technology at Cornell University and a graduate research assistant at the Performance Apparel Lab, with a practice that combines creative exploration and technical ability in large-scale construction, woodworking, garment fabrication, computer programming, and parametric modeling. Most recent projects are exploring fashion, digital fabrication, bio-inspired design, additive manufacturing, traditional making, and material experimentation with reishi.
